## Domain — Mobile Growth & Store Economics

This project ships a consumer mobile application with App Store and/or Google Play acquisition and monetization funnels:
- **Jos** and **Sandy** load **`apple-ads`** for Apple Search Ads placements, Custom Product Pages (CPPs), bidding, and AdAttributionKit / SKAdNetwork measurement.
- **Jos** and **Sandy** load **`google-play-growth`** for Google App Campaigns, Play Console Store Listing Experiments, custom store listings, and Android cohort payback modeling.
- **Leith** and **Gary** align onboarding, trial, and paywall UX with the unit-economic constraints defined by Jos.
